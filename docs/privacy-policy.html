Pitch Shifter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

No data collection

Pitch Shifter does not collect, record, transmit, or store any personal data or audio data. All audio processing is performed entirely on your device using the browser's built-in Web Audio API.

No third parties

The extension does not communicate with any external servers or third-party services. It contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, or remote code of any kind.

Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions solely to perform local audio pitch shifting:

  • tabCapture – captures the active tab's audio stream for local processing when direct element access is unavailable.
  • activeTab / tabs – identifies which tab to pitch-shift and mutes it during capture to prevent audio doubling.
  • scripting – injects a content script to wire page audio elements into a local Web Audio graph.
  • storage – saves your pitch setting per tab within the current browser session only.
  • offscreen – hosts the Web Audio processing context required by the service worker.
  • webNavigation – enumerates sub-frames so embedded media players are also pitch-shifted.
